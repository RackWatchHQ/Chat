# RWS-001 — RackWatch Engineering Specification

**Revision:** 1.0  
**Status:** Developer Issue  
**Document owner:** RackWatch Engineering  
**Product baseline:** Build 1

---

## Document purpose

This document defines the functional behaviour, operational architecture and engineering principles of the RackWatch Build 1 appliance. It specifies what RackWatch shall do and how the product shall behave while avoiding implementation-specific prescription except where an implementation constraint is necessary to preserve product behaviour, interoperability, security or supportability.

RWS-001 is the behavioural authority for Build 1. Detailed physical, electrical and implementation constraints are defined in RWS-002.

## Intended audience

- Software developers and development partners
- Hardware and embedded-system engineers
- Test and integration engineers
- RackWatch product and engineering stakeholders
- Prospective suppliers preparing Build 1 quotations

## Normative language

The terms **shall**, **should** and **may** are used deliberately:

- **shall** — mandatory Build 1 requirement;
- **should** — preferred behaviour or implementation where deviation requires engineering justification;
- **may** — permitted or optional behaviour.
